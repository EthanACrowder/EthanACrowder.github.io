THERMAL / MECHANICAL DESIGN

High Temperature Alumina Flame Disc Nozzle

Designed two alumina ceramic discs for experiments involving high-temperature flames. Single-port and ten-port models were designed. Alumina was chosen for its stability within high-temperature environments. The 0.010" diameter ports should resist enlargement when exposed to elevated temperatures. In addition to temperature resistance, pressure through the small orifices increases the velocity of the combustible gases and extends the flame front away from the surface of the disc.
